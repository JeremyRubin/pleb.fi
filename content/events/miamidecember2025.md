## basic rules

no cheating. submissions should explore scaling frontiers—new ideas welcome, but if it's just "another wrapped btc," we're queuing the jeopardy music. teams up to 5; remote +1 teammate ok if they're more than a couple hours away. rules flex as needed; hit us on telegram for q's.

## why are we charging money?

admission = commitment + croquetas. extra goes to the next event. broke? ask for help. scaling bitcoin shouldn't scale your wallet pain.
